# Eira Motion v2 integration — 2026-11-08

This change is **additive**: the previously shipped `public/assets/characters/eira/eira-flight.png`, its metadata, original canonical frames, and source illustration are unchanged.

## v2 content
- Runtime PNG: `public/assets/characters/eira/eira-motion-v2-atlas-256.png` (2048x1024, RGBA, 256x256 cells)
- Frame JSON: `public/assets/characters/eira/eira-motion-v2.json`
- Idle: 8 subtle float/sway poses, hair pulled closer to the middle
- Slash L: 4 frames, sword clipping corrected
- Slash R: 4 frames, sword edge glow cleaned
- Roll: 12 artistically transformed poses, *not* hand-drawn 360 degree perspective
- Total 28 animation frames in 8-column / 4-row atlas

## Runtime
The game loads the new atlas first; the entire original `eira-flight.png` remains loaded for fallback if v2 fails. In v2 mode, the roll artwork is already tilted and mirrored, so renderer disables its previous 360-degree full-canvas rotation and excessive squashing. Attack sprite index follows the 8-tick swing, four poses at two simulation ticks each. Idle cycles eight poses at 12 FPS.

The two-hit combo timing was committed separately: first slash, second slash eight ticks later, then an 18-tick recovery before the next pair. Projectile fire timing is independent.

## Revert without deleting artwork
To use legacy art again, remove the v2 artwork loader / v2 drawing branch from `src/render/renderer.ts` and redeploy. The legacy files remain at their original paths and are neither overwritten nor regenerated by v2.

## Art limitations
Roll art is a derived lean/mirror motion of the approved Eira pose, not a frame-by-frame illustration of a true axial somersault. Future visual refinement is possible with new approved keyframes.
